# Fidenne troubleshooting guide

Fidenne is meant for a **wired USB DAC** connected directly to the Mac (an external device that turns the sound into analog). Fidenne processes the sound and sends it to built-in speakers · AirPods · HDMI too; macOS mixes the result through its system mixer before sending it to the device, so the data is not bit-identical (**not bit-perfect**). Find your symptom group and try the items from the top. What cannot be fixed is listed in [Known limitations](KNOWN_LIMITATIONS.md). Quoted sentences are what the app shows; **Details** at the end of an item points to the matching Manual section.

## Try these first

1. Check that the DAC is plugged in over USB without a hub and that the amp and DAC volume are up.
2. If the window shows a notice or a red line in its header, do what the sentence says. If the **Lower the volume and start (음량을 낮추고 시작하세요)** notice is up, lower the volume and press **I lowered it, start (낮췄어요, 시작)**.
3. Check that the engine button on the bottom playback bar says **Running (실행 중)** and that the volume of the playing app (Apple Music, Spotify and so on) is not 0.
4. Press **Playback Chain** in the window header and check that the **Output device (출력 장치)** line shows your DAC. If it shows another name or `not in the list` (`목록에 없음`), choose the output DAC again in **I/O · Diagnostics (입출력 · 진단)** in the sidebar.
5. Stop playback, wait a few seconds and play again. If nothing changes, quit Fidenne and open it again. An output format used for the first time on a DAC takes a moment to start.

## 1. No sound

- **Pressing play starts nothing** — a **Lower the volume and start (음량을 낮추고 시작하세요)** (or **Using ○○ for the first time (○○을 처음 씁니다)**) card is up in the Fidenne window. Lower the DAC and amp volume and press **I lowered it, start (낮췄어요, 시작)**. If the window is closed, open it from the menu bar icon.
- **`Sending DSD (DoP) to <DAC> for the first time` (`<DAC>로 DSD(DoP)를 처음 보냅니다`)** — if the DAC manual lists **DoP** support, lower the volume and press **DoP-capable DAC · Start (DoP를 받는 DAC · 시작)**; if unsure, press **Play as PCM 24-bit (PCM 24-bit로 재생)**. Details: [Manual «7.2 DAC format check (formerly "Engine output format")»](MANUAL.md#72-dac-format-check-formerly-engine-output-format)
- **Loud hissing or crackling when starting with DoP** — stop at once, press **Ask again (다시 묻기)** on the DoP line of I/O › DAC format check, then switch the output method to PCM oversampling. That DAC cannot unpack DoP. If the DAC shows DSD but is silent, check that the DAC is not muted in macOS (Fidenne follows the DAC's mute).
- **`Output muted … (출력을 음소거했습니다 …)` in the window header** — the engine's output watchdog blocked a signal that cannot be in music. Stop and play again. If it repeats on the same song, turn EQ, Ostrum and Crossfeed off one at a time to see which one it is, and send a diagnostic. If it appears on ordinary music, turn off **Mute if a signal that is not in music appears (음악에 없는 신호가 나오면 음소거)** in Settings › Sound safety and tell us. Details: [Manual «1. First launch — the five-step first-run setup»](MANUAL.md#1-first-launch--the-five-step-first-run-setup)
- **Silent in PCM Direct** — the engine is bypassed, so check the volume of the playing app and of the DAC.
- **`Another app (…) did not leave the DAC, so exclusive access failed` (`다른 앱(…)이 DAC에서 물러나지 않아 …`)** — stop or quit the app in the parentheses (Spotify, a browser and so on) and play again. Details: [Manual «7.2 DAC format check (formerly "Engine output format")»](MANUAL.md#72-dac-format-check-formerly-engine-output-format)
- **`The saved DAC is not connected` (`저장된 DAC가 연결되지 않았습니다`)** — pick the output DAC again in I/O › Output DAC and refresh (↻). `The selected output device was not found` (`선택한 출력 장치를 찾지 못했습니다`) is the same (after plugging into another USB port).
- **`The system output changed to another device, so PCM Direct playback stopped` (`시스템 출력이 다른 장치로 바뀌어 …`)** — set the macOS sound output back to the DAC and play.
- **Quiet for under 1 second when the engine starts** — normal. The engine gathers sound in a buffer before sending it out. For less, use the **Low latency (저지연)** buffer (less margin against dropouts). Details: [Manual «7.4 Filter · Modulator · Output gain (card) — sound settings for the DSD path»](MANUAL.md#74-filter--modulator--output-gain-card--sound-settings-for-the-dsd-path)
- **The player is playing but `the engine has received exactly 0 for 10 seconds` (`엔진이 받는 소리가 10 초째 완전히 0 입니다`)** — if the input is the system tap, turn Fidenne on in System Settings › Privacy & Security › Screen & System Audio Recording and check the player's volume. If the input is a loopback, check that the Mac's sound output (System Settings › Sound) is that device and that the player has not picked another device (AirPlay, Spotify Connect). Turning the engine off returns other apps' sound to normal. Details: [Manual «7.3 Streaming-app autoplay · Input and output devices»](MANUAL.md#73-streaming-app-autoplay--input-and-output-devices)
- **The spectrum is frozen** — read the first sentence of the line under the DSP graph. `The engine is stopped …` means look at the engine button first; `no sound coming in for N seconds` means the player's output device, permissions and whether it is really playing; `engine-side problem` means turn the engine off and on. If it repeats, send a diagnostic.
- **No Mac sound after you quit Fidenne** — choose the device you were using in System Settings › Sound › Output. Opening Fidenne once more also returns it to the original device.

## 2. Sound cuts out or sounds wrong

- **Sound cuts out** — plug the DAC straight into the Mac, ideally in another USB port, and close other heavy apps. If it still cuts out, switch the output method to **PCM Direct** (it skips processing and is the lightest).
- **`The sound broke up — the output ran empty N times …` (`소리가 끊겼습니다 — 출력이 N번 비었습니다 …`)** — stop, choose a lighter filter or set the buffer to **Stable (안정)**, and start again. For a long filter (ultra-long taps), shorten it to default or long taps. Details: [Manual «7.4 Filter · Modulator · Output gain (card) — sound settings for the DSD path»](MANUAL.md#74-filter--modulator--output-gain-card--sound-settings-for-the-dsd-path)
- **Playback stops with `Trellis processing did not finish in time on this Mac, so playback was stopped …` (`Trellis 처리가 이 Mac 에서 제시간에 끝나지 않아 재생을 멈췄습니다 …`)** — with the conversion method set to Trellis, this Mac could not finish the calculation in time (Trellis is heavy). Press **Choose conversion method (변환 방식 고르기)** next to the notice, switch to **Precise (정밀)**, **Safe (안전)** or CLANS, and play again. If it still stops, lower the output rate to DSD128. Fidenne never changes the method for you. Details: [Manual «7.4»](MANUAL.md#74-filter--modulator--output-gain-card--sound-settings-for-the-dsd-path)
- **One sharp break, then 0.1–0.2 seconds of quiet and it resumes by itself** — the engine refilled an empty queue. A fourth one within a minute stops it and tells you; then do the item above. If the problem line says **short buffer (짧은 버퍼)**, another app changed the output format or the device reconnected — stop and start again.
- **A short break when the track changes or when switching Apple Music ↔ Spotify** — the output is reopened when the track's sample rate changes. A first-time Apple Music track can be quiet for up to 2 seconds until its rate is known. If silence continues, see the "Screen & System Audio Recording" item below.
- **The rate does not match** — the system tap always receives 48 kHz whatever the source. To follow the source rate, use a loopback input (Fidenne Inlet and so on) and turn on Apple Music **automatic source-rate matching (원본 레이트 자동 적용)**. Details: [Manual «7.3 Streaming-app autoplay · Input and output devices»](MANUAL.md#73-streaming-app-autoplay--input-and-output-devices)
- **Controls sound late · sound lags the video** — buffer **Low latency (저지연)** and the default filter lag least. Long and ultra-long taps add filter delay and show `may be out of sync with video` (`영상과 어긋날 수 있음`). Details: [Manual «7.4 Filter · Modulator · Output gain (card) — sound settings for the DSD path»](MANUAL.md#74-filter--modulator--output-gain-card--sound-settings-for-the-dsd-path)
- **Distortion · clipping** — check that the PCM headroom in I/O is **Auto (자동)**, then press **Apply Headroom** as suggested under DSP › Clipping & Headroom, or lower PCM headroom to −6 dB. Details: [Manual «6.2 Equalizer»](MANUAL.md#62-equalizer)
- **EQ · Ostrum · Crossfeed seem not to work** — with the output method **PCM Direct** none of the three is applied; switch to a Fidenne engine output (DSD (DoP) · PCM oversampling). EQ number fields commit only on **Return**, and you must **Save** (⌘S) to keep a change for the next run. Ostrum leaves the sound unchanged while Mix is 0. Details: [Manual «6.2 Equalizer»](MANUAL.md#62-equalizer) · [Manual «6.3 Ostrum»](MANUAL.md#63-ostrum)
- **No curve · target is not shown** — the Headphone and Target layers need a file. If you imported only a measurement, pick a target from **Target (목표)** on the Auto EQ row. A rejection `Line N: …` (`N번째 줄: …`) means fix that line in a text editor. If a curve end is cut off, set the graph axis range to **Auto (자동)**. Details: [Manual «6.5 Headphone ▾ · Target ▾ (the Auto EQ row of Equalizer) · Advanced — files · treble · Generative AI (collapsible)»](MANUAL.md#65-headphone---target--the-auto-eq-row-of-equalizer--advanced--files--treble--generative-ai-collapsible)

## 3. DAC · output

On a DAC or output method used for the first time, start the first playback with the amp and DAC volume low. What happens to the DAC volume with DSD and integer PCM is at the end of this section: [DAC volume and DSD (DoP) · integer PCM](#dac-volume-and-dsd-dop--integer-pcm).

- **In PCM Direct the DAC rate differs from the track's · it stays the same when the track changes** — this is normal. In PCM Direct the sound does not pass through Fidenne, so the DAC rate is not matched track by track but set once at start; macOS converts other rates (Spotify: 44.1 kHz; if the DAC was left at DSD or 352.8 kHz, the track's rate or 44.1 kHz). To process the original rate, use the engine output (DSD (DoP) · PCM 352.8 kHz).
- **The DAC is not visible · the connection was lost** — check the cable and power, plug it in again, press **Reconnect (다시 연결)** or **Search again (다시 찾기)** in the window and play. The switch that resumes playback on the same DAC after a reconnect is in I/O › DAC format check. Details: [Manual «7.2 DAC format check (formerly "Engine output format")»](MANUAL.md#72-dac-format-check-formerly-engine-output-format)
- **`The DAC playback path has stopped inside macOS` (`DAC 재생 통로가 macOS 안에서 멈췄습니다`)** — waiting does not clear it. Unplug the USB cable and plug it in again, or restart Fidenne. Details: [Manual «7.2 DAC format check (formerly "Engine output format")»](MANUAL.md#72-dac-format-check-formerly-engine-output-format)
- **An external USB DAC, but DSD and PCM 352.8 kHz do not start · `does not take 352.8 kHz` (`352.8 kHz 를 받지 않아`)** — for PCM, lower the output rate to one the DAC takes (for example 192 kHz) under `I/O · Diagnostics › Advanced › PCM › Output rate`. For DSD, check two settings on the DAC. ① **USB mode is UAC 2.0** (UAC 1.0 stops at 96 kHz, too low to carry DSD). ② **The DAC's own EQ (PEQ, DSP effects) is off.** An EQ inside the DAC takes PCM and computes it again, so it cannot pass DoP — DSD carried in PCM frames — untouched; many DACs lower the rates they take while their EQ is on (the FiiO K15 drops to 192 kHz). In Audio MIDI Setup the DAC's format list should show 352,800 Hz for the default rate. Use Fidenne's EQ instead; it works together with DSD.
- **The output DAC list shows `PCM Direct only` (`PCM Direct 만`) · `The DAC format table has no row for this format` (`DAC의 형식표에 이 형식을 쓸 행이 없습니다`)** — the device takes none of the engine's PCM rates (44.1 to 768 kHz) or has no 2-channel format (most dongles, some monitors). Listen to Apple Music and Spotify with **PCM Direct**. If playback does not start after you first turn on such a device, choose **PCM Direct** yourself under I/O & Diagnostics › Output mode. 4- and 8-channel interfaces cannot use the engine. Built-in speakers · AirPods · HDMI do not get this mark (below). Details: [Manual «7.1 Independent output control · Output mode»](MANUAL.md#71-independent-output-control--output-mode)
- **On built-in speakers · AirPods · HDMI it says `via system mixer · not bit-perfect`** — Fidenne is processing the sound with EQ · Ostrum · crossfeed · filters and sending it. This is a normal indication that macOS mixes the result through its system mixer before sending it to the device, so the data is not bit-identical. Bit depth and dither cannot be chosen (greyed), and the volume is the Mac's volume.
- **`The device rate changed to … so it restarted at that rate.` · `The device rate changed during playback, so playback stopped. Click Play again.`** — another app changed the rate of a system-mixer device. The first is a notice that it restarted at that rate; for the second click Play again. If `The device kept changing in a short time, so playback stopped.` appears, check the device settings and then click Play.
- **On AirPods, `While the microphone of … is in use, it switches to call quality and Fidenne processing pauses.`** — an app is using the Bluetooth device's microphone. Close it and Fidenne returns by itself with `The call ended, so Fidenne processing restarted.`
- **`Couldn’t set the macOS converter to its highest quality, so audio wasn’t sent to this device.`** — play that device with PCM Direct.
- **The first sound is cut off when the track changes** — the DAC did not have enough time to lock to the new rate. Make `I/O · Diagnostics › Advanced › PCM › Wait when the rate changes` longer (**0.5 s** · **1 s** · **2 s**; per DAC, default 0.25 s). Songs can join **Unchanged** or **Smooth**. These items apply to PCM playback of local files. Details: [Manual «7.1»](MANUAL.md#71-independent-output-control--output-mode)
- **The app opened with PCM Direct** — the format you chose last time is not verified on the current DAC. Verify the DAC and choose the output method again. With playback stopped, press **Check all (모두 확인)** and it comes back straight away from then on. Details: [Manual «7.2 DAC format check (formerly "Engine output format")»](MANUAL.md#72-dac-format-check-formerly-engine-output-format)
- **`This DAC does not accept the player's 44.1 kHz, so it plays at 48 kHz` (`이 DAC는 재생 앱의 44.1 kHz를 받지 않아 48 kHz로 재생합니다`)** — normal. The device accepts only the 48 kHz family and macOS converts.
- **A track moved on by itself but the DAC rate differs from the track** — normal. Playing sound is not touched, so when a track moves on by itself Fidenne does not stop, rewind or change the DAC rate; it carries on at the current rate and macOS converts, so nothing is cut. To process a track at its own rate, pick that track yourself and start it again. Details: [Explanations «Why does the DAC rate stay when a track moves on by itself?»](EXPLANATIONS.md)
- **A `tick` when pausing, seeking or stopping DSD** — processing that reduces the pop at that seam was added. Measured with a test signal it is much smaller, but it has not been confirmed by ear. If you still hear it, report it with the DAC · track · what you pressed ([§9](#9-if-that-still-does-not-work--what-to-collect-and-send)).
- **Fidenne was force-quit during playback and the DAC stayed at a rate such as 352.8 kHz** — the next time you open the app it returns to the earlier rate. If you changed it yourself in Audio MIDI Setup in the meantime, set it back there.

### DAC volume and DSD (DoP) · integer PCM

This applies only to Fidenne's engine output (DSD (DoP) · PCM oversampling). PCM Direct is not affected.

**Affected: a DAC whose volume control is linked to the macOS volume.** In System Settings › Sound › Output, pick the DAC: if its volume bar moves, it is affected; if the bar is grey or missing, it is not (Audio MIDI Setup shows the same). A DAC or amplifier knob does not count unless the macOS volume bar moves with it.

What Fidenne does with such a DAC:

- When playback starts, Fidenne first lowers the sound inside Fidenne by the DAC's volume, then sets the DAC side to its maximum and sends the signal bit for bit. What you hear stays as loud as before. The output device line in Playback Chain shows something like `DAC 음량 −24 dB 를 Fidenne 이 넘겨받음` (Fidenne took over the DAC volume of −24 dB).
- When playback ends, the DAC volume goes back to where it was. If the app quit suddenly, it goes back the next time you open Fidenne.
- If that volume changes during playback (Audio MIDI Setup, a volume app, a linked knob), Fidenne mutes for a moment and takes it over again. During playback the DAC side sits at its maximum and cannot go higher; to listen louder, stop, raise the volume, and play again.
- If it cannot take the volume over, it stops and says `이 DAC 의 음량을 Fidenne 이 넘겨받지 못해 …` (Fidenne could not take over this DAC's volume …). Listen to that DAC with PCM Direct.
- If another app changes the DAC's output format during playback, Fidenne stops and says `DAC 의 출력 형식이 재생 중 바뀌어 …` (the DAC's output format changed during playback …). Play again.
- The **DAC volume (DAC 음량)** slider in the bottom bar reads and changes the DAC's own volume. During DSD · integer output Fidenne takes over the DAC volume as above and holds it at maximum, so the slider is dimmed and you use the DAC's own knob. Hover to see why it is dimmed: `이 DAC 는 본체 노브로 조절합니다` (this DAC is adjusted with its own knob; a DAC with no volume control) · `이 DAC의 음량 속성은 읽기 전용입니다` (read-only) · `출력 상태를 확인하는 동안 음량을 조절할 수 없습니다` (cannot adjust while the output state is being checked). If `DAC 음량을 바꾸지 못했습니다. 장치에서 읽은 값을 표시합니다` appears, the slider shows the value the device reported; set the volume on the DAC itself.

Not affected: the keyboard volume keys while you listen through Fidenne Inlet (Inlet volume keys follow a supported output device’s hardware volume, or show why they cannot), the playing app's volume (Music, Spotify, a browser — that is the level before Fidenne receives the sound, so it has nothing to do with DoP), and the paths by which Fidenne takes other apps' sound in general.

Before the first playback, start with the DAC and amplifier volume low.

## 4. Music services

- **A service will not connect** — check that you are online. If you did not allow internet access at first setup, or Spotify, album art or squig.link do not work, turn the destination on in Settings › Privacy › Network destinations (네트워크 목적지). If the service shows `Not connected` (`연결 안 됨`) press **Connect… (연결하기…)**; if only the lists are empty, refresh.
- **Play and track-skip buttons do nothing · `control failed` (`제어에 실패했습니다`)** — allow Fidenne to control Music and Spotify in System Settings › Privacy & Security › Automation. The first-play permission window is waited for until you answer it. If you passed it without allowing, you can turn it on in the same place later; after you return to Fidenne the `…접근을 허용해 주세요` (please allow access) notice goes away and the lists are read again. Before the first-run setup is over nothing is sent to Music or Spotify, so having no permission prompt until then is normal. Details: [Manual «3. Streaming page»](MANUAL.md#3-streaming-page)
- **`Apple Music did not answer in time` (`Apple Music이 정해진 시간 안에 답하지 않았습니다`)** — the library is very large. Open it again a little later (it is not a permissions problem).
- **I played one app and the other stopped** — this is normal. The engine follows one streaming app at a time, so only the one started later stays. A connected YouTube Music tab is coordinated too; other browser sound cannot be controlled and may be heard together. Details: [Manual «7.3 Streaming-app autoplay · Input and output devices»](MANUAL.md#73-streaming-app-autoplay--input-and-output-devices)
- **Another track slips in at the end of an Apple Music track, or a `Fidenne 재생 대기열` playlist appeared in Music** — **Hand tracks over via a Fidenne playlist (곡 넘김을 Fidenne 플레이리스트로)** in Settings › Privacy › Apple Music is on. Fidenne puts all the remaining tracks of the list into that playlist, so with Music's autoplay (∞) on, no other track slips in before the list ends. If one still does, turn off autoplay (∞). Details: [Manual «8. Settings Window»](MANUAL.md#8-settings-window)
- **I chose an Apple Music playlist but the old track goes on** — a list read for the first time is read to the end before playing. If a notice appears, choose it again or press **Refresh list (목록 새로고침)** (↻).
- **Spotify `Keychain permission needed` (`키체인 허용 필요`)** — the login keychain is locked. It is read again automatically a moment later, or press **Read from keychain again (키체인에서 다시 읽기)** or **Connect**.
- **`Spotify asks you to rest for a moment` (`Spotify 가 잠시 쉬라고 합니다`)** — Spotify's request limit. Try again after the time shown. Details: [Manual «3. Streaming page»](MANUAL.md#3-streaming-page)
- **Spotify covers are grey** — covers are fetched one song at a time, and a song whose cover could not be fetched stays a grey placeholder and is tried again. If it lasts, check that Spotify is connected in Settings, and disconnect and connect again. Playback and the library keep working without the cover. Closing the login window part-way through connecting does not erase an already-connected account.
- **I pressed a song in the search results and it played at once without asking how** — that is normal. When Fidenne is not running a list, it does not ask and plays the song at once. It asks **how to play (재생 방법)** only when a list is running.
- **Spotify is missing from search results** — it shows only in the **Spotify** tab. Press the tab with the same search term.
- **`앨범 저장 권한을 허용하려면 Spotify에 다시 연결해 주세요.`** — the earlier connection was made without the permission to save albums. In the Spotify section of Settings press **Connect Spotify (Spotify 연결)** again and allow it.
- **After disconnecting Spotify, the Spotify tracks in listening history are gone** — normal. Disconnecting asks for confirmation and then deletes tokens immediately and hides Spotify information on this Mac (Spotify tracks in listening history, Fidenne lists, track links and caches) for four days before deleting it. Reconnect within four days to restore remaining data, or use **Delete all now** to erase it immediately. Tracks heard through Spotify are never counted in the listening statistics.
- **`Spotify 연결이 막혀 보관함 연동을 멈췄습니다. 소리 처리는 계속됩니다.`** (Spotify connection was blocked; library features stopped, sound processing continues) — Spotify turned this connection away. Sound processing of tracks from the Spotify app goes on; only search and the library stop. Check the key (Client ID), press **Check key**, and connect again.
- **I saved an Apple Music album but it does not show in Music** — if you saw `보관함에 넣었습니다. Music 앱에 보이기까지 조금 걸릴 수 있습니다.`, the request was accepted and Apple is slow to reflect it. Refresh Music after a few minutes. If the iCloud library is off you see `iCloud 보관함이 꺼져 있어 Music에서 열어 주세요.` (iCloud library is off; open in Music); turn on the iCloud library in the Music app's settings and try again. `아직 Music 에 보이지 않습니다` (not in Music yet) is not a failure; it means it could not be confirmed yet.

## 5. YouTube Music

- **A track does not show on the bar · the bar buttons do not move the tab** — turn on **Connect with browser extensions (Safari · Chrome · Edge) (브라우저 확장과 연결 (Safari · Chrome · Edge))** in Settings › Privacy › YouTube Music. If the line says `On · no connected browser` (`켜짐 · 연결된 브라우저 없음`) press **Installation guide… (설치 안내…)** and follow its four steps. Multiple browsers can be connected.
  1. In the guide window choose your browser, press **① Copy address**, paste it in the address bar and press Return (`chrome://extensions` · `edge://extensions`).
  2. Turn on **② Developer mode** and press the button that appears (**Load unpacked** / **압축해제된 확장 프로그램을 로드합니다**).
  3. Press **Copy path (경로 복사)**, then in the folder window press ⌘⇧G → ⌘V → Return → **Open**. The folder to pick is the copied `Chrome` folder itself.
  4. Reload the already-open YouTube Music tab with ⌘R. When the bottom line of the guide window says **1 browser connected (브라우저 1개 연결됨)** you are done.
  5. **Tab sleep exception**: if the browser puts unused tabs to sleep, YouTube Music cannot answer. In Chrome add `music.youtube.com` under Settings › Performance › Memory Saver › **Always keep these sites active**; in Edge under Settings › System and performance › Resource saving › **Never put these sites to sleep**.
  If you moved or updated the app, turn the switch off and on once. There is no track information during ads. Details: [Manual «8.1 Using YouTube Music (Chrome extension)»](MANUAL.md#81-using-youtube-music-chrome-extension)
- **`The extension in the browser is an old version` (`브라우저의 확장이 옛 판입니다`)** — on the browser's extensions page press refresh (↻) on the Fidenne for YouTube Music card and reload the tab with ⌘R. When the result cannot be confirmed Fidenne does not press again (it could undo the save), so check in YouTube Music. With an old extension only `YouTube Music에서 열기` shows instead of a save button; reload the extension as above.

(2) In Safari › Settings › **Extensions** check that Fidenne's YouTube Music extension is on, then on the music.youtube.com tab press the Fidenne icon in the row of extension icons next to Safari's address bar and always allow it on this website. If the line still says «Safari 확장이 응답하지 않습니다» (the Safari extension is not answering), check that the Fidenne toolbar icon has allowed the site, then reload that tab with ⌘R. If the Safari card or Settings › Privacy › YouTube Music says `Safari 확장은 Fidenne을 응용 프로그램 폴더로 옮긴 뒤 다시 열어야 Safari에 보입니다.` (move Fidenne to the Applications folder and open it again for the extension to appear in Safari), Fidenne was opened from inside the .dmg or from the Downloads folder — quit it, move the app to the **Applications (응용 프로그램)** folder and open it from there. Details: [Manual «8.1 Using YouTube Music (Chrome extension)»](MANUAL.md#81-using-youtube-music-chrome-extension)

The screen where a track plays and album screens are not read. If only part arrives, scroll to the end. Details: [Manual «4.1 Importing YouTube Music playlists»](MANUAL.md#41-importing-youtube-music-playlists)

## 6. Screen · app

- **The app will not open** — Fidenne opens only on macOS 15 or later on an Apple silicon Mac. It does not open on an Intel Mac. Check the version in the Apple menu › About This Mac.
- **A `시작 복구` (Startup recovery) window appears as soon as I open it** — it appears on the third start after two starts in a row did not finish. No setting is erased. **안전하게 시작** (start safely) enters for this one run with the engine · capture · browser extension · auto-play connections off (`안전 시작 중`, safe start, is shown); check your settings there and reopen the app to return to normal. **진단 만들기…** (make diagnostics) can build the material to send first, and **그대로 시작** (start as usual) is for when nothing looks wrong. If it keeps repeating, make diagnostics and report it ([§9](#9-if-that-still-does-not-work--what-to-collect-and-send)).
- **On macOS 15 the screen material looks different or the Apple Intelligence translation line is dimmed** — normal. The Liquid Glass effects and Apple Intelligence translation are macOS 26 features, so on macOS 15 Fidenne uses the earlier surface material and the other translation routes. The Liquid Glass box in Settings › General also says `이 macOS에서는 반투명 효과로 대신 그립니다.` (translucent materials are drawn instead). On macOS 15 the playback capsule is always opaque so it stands apart from the window, and the capsule **material (재질)** picker in Settings is not shown (it returns on macOS 26 or later).
- **No Fidenne icon in the menu bar** — turn on the Fidenne switch in System Settings › Menu Bar › Allow in the Menu Bar. If it is on but not visible, the menu bar is full and pushed it out. Open the window from the Dock. Details: [Manual «2.4 Menu bar icon · menus»](MANUAL.md#24-menu-bar-icon--menus)
- **No Screen page in the sidebar** — settings were gathered in one window. Open it with ⌘, · Settings… in the app menu · **Settings…** in the sidebar.
- **I cannot turn EQ · Ostrum · Crossfeed on and off in Playback Chain** — a chip is a button that opens a panel, and turning it on and off is done with the switch beside the name in the panel's first row. If the switch is dimmed, the reason is shown next to it — you are listening in PCM Direct, or the engine reports no values. Details: [Manual «2.1 Page header»](MANUAL.md#21-page-header)
- **The screen looks small on a big monitor · text grows when I enlarge the window** — when **Grow automatically in a large window (큰 창에서 자동으로 키우기)** in Settings › General is on, the whole screen grows together up to 1.5 times once the window is larger than 1440×900. Turn that switch off if you do not want it. `지금 N%` below it is the current scale.
- **The Playback Chain column is missing on the large playback screen** — its edge handle (`‹`) is folded. Press it or ⌥⌘P. Details: [Manual «2.2 Bottom playback bar»](MANUAL.md#22-bottom-playback-bar)
- **Two-finger scrolling over a slider does not change the value** — scroll **while holding ⌘**. To change this use Settings › Controls and shortcuts; if a shortcut does nothing, check the shortcut list there. Details: [Manual «2.6 Handling knobs · sliders · EQ points»](MANUAL.md#26-handling-knobs--sliders--eq-points)
- **Swiping sideways does not go back or forward** — it follows System Settings › Trackpad › More Gestures › Swipe between pages. The **‹** button above the list does the same. Details: [Manual «3. Streaming page»](MANUAL.md#3-streaming-page)
- **Control Center shows `No playback information`** — Fidenne posts only when it knows the player's track. Browser sound is posted by the browser itself.

## 7. Installation · permissions

- **Installing Fidenne Inlet** — press **Install… (설치…)** in I/O · Diagnostics › Input and output devices and enter your password. Afterwards press ↻ to read again. If `The device is not visible yet` (`장치가 아직 보이지 않습니다`) remains, restart the Mac. All sound breaking for a moment during install or removal is normal. `Cancelled` (`취소했습니다`) means you cancelled the password window and nothing changed. When a new Inlet version is available, the home screen tells you while nothing is playing; press **Update… (업데이트…)** in that notice or in the same place. Details: [Manual «7.3 Streaming-app autoplay · Input and output devices»](MANUAL.md#73-streaming-app-autoplay--input-and-output-devices)
- **The install stopped** — `Another file exists` and `The install folder has a file that is not Fidenne's, so it stopped` mean the place it installs to already holds a file with the same name; Fidenne does not delete it, so check it yourself. `Another install is in progress` → press again once it is done. `Could not get the install lock` → restart the Mac and press again.
- **Microphone permission** — `Fidenne Inlet input permission is missing` (`Fidenne Inlet 입력 권한이 없습니다`) → allow Fidenne in System Settings › Privacy & Security › Microphone. `Fidenne Inlet is missing, so receiving through the system tap` (`Fidenne Inlet 이 없어 시스템 탭으로 받습니다`) means it is not installed; do **Install…** above.
- **System tap · Screen & System Audio Recording** — macOS asks on first start. If you refused, turn Fidenne on in System Settings › Privacy & Security › Screen & System Audio Recording. Turning the engine off returns that sound to normal.
- **A device of another loopback app is not in the input list** — pick it yourself with **+ Another input device (+ 다른 입력 장치)** under the input. A device whose input is not 2 channels is greyed out with the reason. Virtual devices are not listed as output DACs (normal).
- **Removing Fidenne** — first press **Remove…** for Fidenne Inlet under I/O · Diagnostics › Input and output devices. Moving only the app to the Trash leaves the Inlet device behind. If you used the YouTube Music extension, turn off "Connect with browser extensions (Safari · Chrome · Edge)" under Settings › Privacy › YouTube Music and remove the extension from the browser. Then quit the app and move Fidenne from Applications to the Trash. To remove settings and history too, use Finder › Go › Go to Folder and delete `~/Library/Application Support/Fidenne` · `~/Library/Caches/io.github.fidenne` · `~/Library/HTTPStorages/io.github.fidenne` · `~/Library/Preferences/io.github.fidenne.plist`, and delete the items found for `io.github.fidenne` in Keychain Access. If the app is already gone, delete `/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/HAL/FidenneLoopback.driver` and restart the Mac.
- **After switching to a new version macOS asks for permissions again** — the app identifier changed, so macOS sees a new app. Press **Allow** once each. Settings, listening history and library stay. If a keychain access window appears, press **Allow**.

## 8. Other symptoms

- **A local file will not play** — the status on the library track row is the reason (`Unsupported file type`, `The file is damaged`, `File not found`). If `This DAC does not accept ○○ kHz N-bit integer PCM …` appears, change the output rate to another one the DAC takes or change the bit depth; if that fails, choose DSD (DoP). Details: [Manual «4. Library page»](MANUAL.md#4-local-page)
- **Library warning `No write permission: data folder (…)`** — Fidenne could not write to its data folder (`~/Library/Application Support/Fidenne`). In Finder › Get Info set your permission to **Read & Write** and press **Reindex (다시 색인)**. The same applies when a folder cannot be removed from the library. Details: [Manual «4. Library page»](MANUAL.md#4-local-page)
- **An external disk shows fewer tracks than files** — files whose names start with `.` and hidden folders are not counted. Zero-byte and truncated files show as `damaged file`; download or delete them again.
- **An older song is missing from the Lyrics tab** — the Lyrics tab collects songs whose lyrics have been shown. A lyric you saved earlier comes up once you play that song again (lyrics internet search must be on). Also check that the service chip is All and the search field is empty. Details: [Manual «2.3 Lyrics»](MANUAL.md#23-lyrics)
- **Lyrics do not show** — turn on lrclib.net search in the sidebar's **Lyrics** tab › **Lyrics source (가사 출처)**, or attach an LRC or TXT file. For another version use **Other version (다른 판)** in the source line under the lyrics, then pick it in **Lyrics version (가사 판)**. Details: [Manual «2.3 Lyrics»](MANUAL.md#23-lyrics)
- **`가사 서버(LRCLIB)가 요청을 잠시 거부했습니다…`** (the lyrics server refused requests for a while) — the lyrics server blocked requests for a moment after many in a short time. For the number of minutes shown Fidenne makes no new request. Wait, or turn lyrics search off and on in the sidebar's **Lyrics** tab › **Lyrics source** to resume at once. Meanwhile an LRC · TXT file can be connected.
- **AI lyrics translation does not appear** — in the sidebar's **Lyrics** tab › **Translation steps (번역 단계)** › **AI provider settings** do what the sentence says: `API 키를 저장해 주세요.` (save an API key) · `제공자와 모델을 선택하고 키를 저장해 주세요.` · `키체인이 잠겨 있습니다. 잠금을 푼 뒤 키 상태를 다시 확인하세요.` (keychain is locked) · `API 키 접근이 거부되었습니다. 키체인 접근 권한을 확인하세요.` · `API 키와 모델 사용 권한을 확인하세요.` (the account does not allow the key or model) · `제공자의 요청 제한에 도달했습니다. 나중에 직접 다시 시도하세요.` (provider limit) · `선택한 오늘 요청 상한에 도달했습니다.` (today's limit) · `AI 번역 보관함이 256 MiB에 도달했습니다. 새로 만들려면 번역을 직접 지워 주세요.` (storage full) · `LRCLIB 가사를 새로 불러온 뒤 AI 번역을 사용하세요.` (load the lyrics from LRCLIB first). When it cannot be used, Fidenne does not switch to another provider; it shows the Apple model on this Mac or macOS translation, and last the original. The key is only in the login keychain and **키 삭제** (delete key) in Settings removes it. Details: [Manual «8. Settings Window»](MANUAL.md#8-settings-window)
- **A YouTube Music choice says `Could not save the choice and reverted it`** — the library file that records choices could not be written. Press again a little later; if it continues, check the disk space. `Files not read` in an import means download the takeout again. Details: [Manual «4.1 Importing YouTube Music playlists»](MANUAL.md#41-importing-youtube-music-playlists)
- **Nothing in Listening History** — check that the track played for over 30 seconds (or half the track) and that **Save listening history on this Mac** is on. To clear it use **Clear listening history** under history settings. Details: [Manual «5. Listening History page»](MANUAL.md#5-listening-history-page)
- **`Check for updates…` is greyed out** — this build has nowhere to check. `No public release yet` is not a failure. `The github.com connection is off` → turn it on under Network destinations. Details: [Manual «2.4 Menu bar icon · menus»](MANUAL.md#24-menu-bar-icon--menus)
- **`Could not read the saved DSP settings` · `Could not read my presets` · `Could not read the EQ presets`** — the record is broken or written by a newer app. The original is kept and the engine starts with defaults and an empty list. Save again (⌘S) to store a new one. Details: [Manual «6.2 Equalizer»](MANUAL.md#62-equalizer)

- **A plug-in is off** — the card on the Plug-in page gives the reason: `It could not keep up and was turned off` · `It stopped and was turned off` · `It does not accept this track's sample rate, so it is off` · `It cannot be used as stereo, so it is off` · `Its delay is too large, so it is off this time` · `It produced invalid sound and was turned off` · `The sound got too loud, so it was turned off`. Press **Turn Back On** or **Reload** on the card. A plug-in missing from the list is one that did not pass validation (`Only plug-ins that passed validation can be used`) or `It is not an effect plug-in`. If a line says to restart, quit and reopen Fidenne. Details: [Manual «6.9»](MANUAL.md#69-plug-in-page)
- **An EQ file cannot be imported** — `No Preamp or Filter lines found …` means it is not REW · Equalizer APO · AutoEq filter text; for `Line N couldn’t be read, so none of the file was imported.`, fix that line and import again. Notices such as filter types this EQ does not have, out-of-range values and filters written without a Q mean only those filters were skipped; values are never changed. After saving, the sound does not change until you choose it in **My EQ**. Details: [Manual «6.2»](MANUAL.md#62-equalizer)
- **English · Japanese sentences look awkward** — the translations are AI drafts that no native speaker has reviewed. Tell us which ones. The screen language follows macOS's language order.
- **The pronunciation line is wrong** — the pronunciation is macOS's estimate and kanji readings or sound changes can be wrong. Click the line in the Lyrics tab to edit it.

## 9. If that still does not work — what to collect and send

1. Open the menu **Help › Create diagnostics for a problem report… (도움말 › 문제 제보용 진단 만들기…)**. If you agreed to the optional diagnostic record at first launch, it is already recording: make the bundle right after the problem, before quitting the app. If it is off, press **Start diagnostic recording (진단 기록 시작)** and make the bundle the next time the problem happens.
2. Press **Create bundle and view contents (번들 만들고 내용 보기)** to see what will be sent. The bundle is saved as a .zip file in your Downloads folder. It contains times, services, rates and random per-track labels for that period; it does not contain track titles, artists, albums, file paths, device names or accounts.
3. Press **Send by email (메일로 보내기)**. Your mail app opens. If you use another mail service (Gmail, Naver and so on), press **Copy mail form (메일 양식 복사)** and paste the address, subject and form. Attach the .zip file from your Downloads folder and send it to **fidenne.support@gmail.com**. Do not copy and paste the contents: they get cut off by length limits, and the records from when the problem happened may be lost. If you cannot find the file, open "Downloads" in Finder. The app sends nothing by itself.
4. At the moment something goes wrong, press **⌥⌃⌘K** (menu **Help › Mark That Something Was Off (방금 이상했음 표시)**). That time is marked in the diagnostic record, so the part is easy to find in the bundle you send. It is marked even if you did not agree to the diagnostic record.
5. In the email write in a line or two what happened. The app version and macOS version are already inside the diagnostic bundle.
